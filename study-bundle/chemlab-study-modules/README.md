# ChemLab — Complete Engineering Chemistry Study Bundle (Modules 1–4, v2)

This updated archive incorporates the full **Module 7: Thermochemistry and Energy** lecture notes alongside **Module 9: Electrochemistry**, **Module 1 (Lab Safety)**, and **Module 2 (Basic Chemical Concepts)** for **ChemLab**:

1. **`module-01-intro-and-lab-safety.md`** — Engineering relevance of chemistry, lab conduct & hygiene, laboratory glassware & apparatus, chemical storage & GHS hazard pictograms, PPE selection, and chemical waste segregation & spill response.
2. **`module-02-basic-chemical-concepts.md`** — Classification of matter (elements, compounds, mixtures), states & phase changes, SI units & prefixes, significant figures & rounding, scientific notation, accuracy vs. precision, dimensional analysis, the mole & molar mass, stoichiometry, and solution concentration.
3. **`module-03-thermochemistry-and-equilibrium.md`** — System, surroundings & universe (open/closed/isolated), state vs. path functions, First Law of Thermodynamics ($\Delta U = q + w$), heat ($q = mc\Delta T$, $q = C\Delta T$), pressure-volume work ($w = -P\Delta V$, $1\text{ L}\cdot\text{atm} = 101.3\text{ J}$), enthalpy ($\Delta H$) & thermochemical equation scaling, coffee-cup calorimetry, Hess's law, average bond energies ($\Delta H \approx \sum E_{\text{broken}} - \sum E_{\text{formed}}$), dynamic equilibrium ($K_c$, $Q$ vs. $K$), and Le Chatelier's principle.
4. **`module-04-electrochemistry.md`** — Oxidation-reduction (`OIL RIG`), oxidation number rules, balancing redox equations in acidic and basic media, galvanic vs. electrolytic cells, salt bridges & cell notation, standard reduction potentials ($E^\circ_{\text{cell}}$), Gibbs free energy ($\Delta G^\circ = -nFE^\circ_{\text{cell}}$), Nernst equation, and Faraday's laws of electrolysis.
5. **`formula-and-constants-sheet.md`** — Consolidated quick-reference sheet of physical constants, equations, sign conventions, and safety rules across all 4 modules.
6. **`chemlab-curriculum.json`** — Machine-readable structured dataset of lessons, worked examples, key tables, and practice questions ready for direct ingestion into `Projects/chemlab/`.

# ChemLab — Master Formula & Constants Sheet (Modules 1–4)

## Key Equations by Module
- **Module 2 (Stoichiometry & Solutions):**
  - Moles: $n = \frac{m}{M} = \frac{N}{N_A}$
  - Molarity: $M = \frac{n_{\text{solute}}}{V_{\text{solution (L)}}}$
  - Dilution: $C_1 V_1 = C_2 V_2$
- **Module 3 (Thermochemistry & Equilibrium):**
  - First Law of Thermodynamics: $\Delta U = q + w$
  - Sensible Heat: $q = m c \Delta T = C \Delta T$
  - Pressure-Volume Work: $w = -P \Delta V$
  - Coffee-Cup Calorimetry: $q_{\text{reaction}} = -q_{\text{solution}}$
  - Bond Energy Estimate: $\Delta H \approx \sum E_{\text{broken}} - \sum E_{\text{formed}}$
  - Equilibrium Constant: $K_c = \frac{[\text{C}]^c[\text{D}]^d}{[\text{A}]^a[\text{B}]^b}$ (omit pure solids & liquids)
- **Module 4 (Electrochemistry):**
  - Standard Cell Voltage: $E^\circ_{\text{cell}} = E^\circ_{\text{cathode}} - E^\circ_{\text{anode}}$
  - Standard Free Energy: $\Delta G^\circ = -n F E^\circ_{\text{cell}}$
  - Nernst Equation ($25^\circ\text{C}$): $E_{\text{cell}} = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - \frac{0.0592\text{ V}}{n}\log_{10} Q$
  - Electrolysis Mass: $m = \frac{I \cdot t \cdot M}{n \cdot F}$
